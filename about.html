How I got here

Changing direction is allowed.

For a long time I was going to be a doctor. Then I did a two-week internship at Kantha Bopha, and somewhere in those two weeks I realized I wasn't as keen on it as I'd thought.

That was a strange thing to admit after saying it out loud for years. I decided to treat it as a lesson: go and look for another path, and get to know myself a bit better on the way.

High school has mostly been that. I try things, I notice how they feel, and when something feels wrong I change direction. That's how I ended up here, wanting to study Software Engineering.

If I had to pick one strength, it's adaptability.

Five years from now

A morning run, the gym, a matcha, a healthy breakfast, then off to my own tech company, building things that help the people around me and make a real difference in my community. And, honestly, making a living from them too.
