02Tested

KiriShield

An AI-powered Telegram bot that helps Cambodian elderly users spot scam messages and content that may be AI-generated.

  • Telegram bot
  • Google Gemini
  • API integration
01Problem

Scam messages, fake links and AI-generated videos and pictures are getting harder to tell apart from the real thing, and elderly people in Cambodia are often the ones targeted.

Did something specific make you start this? A family member, a story you heard?

02Idea

A bot you can forward something suspicious to (a text, a link, a video, a picture or a file) and get back a clear risk check.

03Design

Why Telegram? How did you design the replies so they're easy for older users to understand?

04Development
  • Integrated Google's Gemini model to analyse messages
  • Built the API client integration
  • Fixed crashes caused by Telegram's entity parsing
  • Tested it against a range of real scam scenarios

Test results: risk score per scenario

Lottery / prize scam~98%
Fake ABA Bank identity update~98%
E-commerce job scam~95%
Mobile top-up reward scam~95%
Legitimate family message0%
05Challenges

Telegram's message formatting kept crashing the bot until the entity parsing was fixed. Phishing links were another one: the bot needed to read the link's domain properly before it could judge it, and that took some work to get right.
